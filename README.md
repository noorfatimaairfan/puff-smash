<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>
  The game Puff Smash is a 2D action game which aims at fighting the attacking
  monsters in the townsville of Powerpuff Girls. Powerpuff Girls are well known
  superheroes who fight monsters and save the city. The player gets to decide
  between three characters each possessing special powers. The player can fire
  projectiles around the screen to kill the monster. As the heroes land hit the
  monster, their power grows making their attack stronger. The game keeps a
  running tally of wins for the heroes and the monsters.
</p>

<h2>Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Apply core C++ concepts and use SFML for graphics, texts, keyboard input, timing and sound.</li>
  <li>Implement collision detection between a projectile, monsters, and heroes.</li>
  <li>Design a colorful interface with character selection to enhance user engagement.</li>
  <li>Develop a power growth system and track game results and damage.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Features and Functionalities</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Different Powers:</b> Three heroes each with different attack powers.</li>
  <li><b>Moving Enemies:</b> The monster moves forward across the screen adding pressure to every fight.</li>
  <li><b>Movement and Shooting:</b> Space bar to shoot, arrow keys to move the hero, P to pause, Esc to exit.</li>
  <li><b>Health Bars:</b> For the hero and the monsters.</li>
  <li><b>Score Bar:</b> Tracks points for every hit.</li>
  <li><b>Round Timer:</b> Adds pressure to every fight.</li>
  <li><b>Interactive Interface:</b> An intro screen, character selection screen, result screen.</li>
  <li><b>Difficulty Level:</b> Heroes gain power at every hit and monster levels up every 30 seconds.</li>
  <li><b>Sound Effects and Background Music:</b> For attacks and hits.</li>
  <li><b>Praise Pop-ups:</b> Words like “BRAVO”, “SUPERB”, and “GREAT KILL” appear on every successful hit.</li>
</ul>
